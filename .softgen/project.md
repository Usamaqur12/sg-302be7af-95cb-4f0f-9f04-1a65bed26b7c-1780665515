# Multi-Vendor Marketplace Platform

## Vision
Amazon-style marketplace connecting customers, sellers, and platform admin. Customers discover and purchase from multiple vendors, sellers manage products and orders, admin oversees the entire ecosystem with commission-based revenue model.

## Design
Industrial commerce meets editorial refinement — logistics terminal precision with artisan marketplace soul.

**Color System:**
- `--primary: 222 47% 11%` (slate-900 — industrial authority)
- `--accent: 14 80% 58%` (terracotta — earned warmth, CTAs/deals only)
- `--background: 40 40% 97%` (cream-50 — softens digital edge)
- `--foreground: 222 47% 11%` (slate-900 text)
- `--muted: 220 13% 91%` (slate-200)
- `--card: 0 0% 100%` (white cards)
- `--destructive: 0 84% 60%` (red for alerts)
- Amber (45 93% 47%) for semantic warnings/deals

**Typography:**
- Heading: IBM Plex Sans (600, 700 weights)
- Body: IBM Plex Sans (400, 500 weights)
- Editorial: Source Serif 4 (600 weight for seller stories, product highlights)
- Data: IBM Plex Mono (tabular-nums for prices, analytics)

**Style:** Dense grids for dashboards, airy editorial for storefront, staggered product cards, purposeful hover lifts.

## Features

**Customer Portal:**
- Browse products, categories, search/filter
- Cart, checkout with payment
- Order tracking, history
- Reviews, ratings, wishlist
- Account dashboard, returns/refunds

**Seller Dashboard:**
- Registration, KYC verification
- Store profile management
- Product CRUD, inventory, pricing
- Order processing, shipping updates
- Earnings dashboard, withdrawal requests
- Customer messages, reviews

**Admin Dashboard:**
- User, seller, product approval
- Category management
- Order, payment, payout oversight
- Commission settings
- Coupons, banners, content management
- Analytics, reports
- Support tickets, notifications
- Shipping/tax configuration
