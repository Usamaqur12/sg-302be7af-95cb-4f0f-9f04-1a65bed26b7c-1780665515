## Objective

Build the operating plan for sourcing, qualifying, onboarding, activating, and monitoring the first Mercato launch vendor cohort.

Launch target:

- Recruit 10-20 approved launch vendors.
- Approve 100-300 launch-ready SKUs.
- Prioritize sellers who can fulfill reliably, provide clear product content, respond quickly, and comply with Mercato trust standards.
- Keep launch scope controlled: manually managed outreach, onboarding, documents, catalog review, test orders, and go-live approval are acceptable for MVP.

## Launch Vendor ICP

Ideal launch vendors are small to mid-sized sellers with existing customer demand, clear inventory ownership, and the ability to respond within one business day.

Priority vendor profiles:

- Social commerce sellers already selling through Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, WhatsApp, or local marketplace groups.
- Small retail shops with stable inventory in electronics accessories, beauty, fashion basics, home goods, fitness, gifts, toys, stationery, or lifestyle categories.
- Niche product specialists with strong images, clear product knowledge, and repeatable sourcing.
- Referral vendors from known shop owners, community groups, existing buyers, local suppliers, and category operators.
- Existing marketplace sellers who want another controlled sales channel and can comply with quality and fulfillment expectations.

Avoid for launch:

- Vendors with unclear product ownership, counterfeit risk, inconsistent pricing, or stock uncertainty.
- Vendors who cannot provide basic identity/business details, payout information, product images, or return handling commitments.
- Vendors whose catalog depends on restricted goods, fragile fulfillment, regulated claims, or high dispute risk unless leadership explicitly approves the category.
- Vendors who require custom technical integrations before launch.

## Qualification Rubric

Score each prospect from 1-5 in each category. A launch vendor should average at least 4.0 and must not score below 3 in trust, fulfillment, or responsiveness.

| Area | Weight | 5-point standard | Reject signal |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Trust and legitimacy | 25% | Clear seller identity, known selling history, real business or social storefront, no obvious counterfeit or policy risk | Refuses identity/business details, copied brand claims, suspicious sourcing |
| Fulfillment readiness | 25% | Owns or controls stock, can pack and dispatch on agreed timeline, can support test order | No stock certainty, cannot quote dispatch time, repeated fulfillment excuses |
| Catalog quality | 20% | Products have accurate names, prices, variants, images, descriptions, stock counts, and return notes | Poor images, vague specs, misleading claims, no SKU discipline |
| Responsiveness | 15% | Replies within one business day during onboarding, completes checklist without repeated chasing | Slow replies before launch, missed meetings, incomplete forms |
| Category fit | 15% | Adds depth to launch categories and has 5-30 credible SKUs for initial listing | Duplicative low-quality assortment, restricted or high-risk category |

Qualification decision:

- Approve for launch cohort: score 4.0+ average, no hard reject signal, ready to complete onboarding in 7 days.
- Nurture for post-launch: promising seller but missing content, documents, stock confidence, or timing.
- Reject: trust risk, prohibited products, inability to fulfill, or unwillingness to follow Mercato policies.

Required source channels:

- WhatsApp seller groups, referrals, and community commerce groups.
- Small shops and local retailers with existing product photography.
- Existing social marketplace pages with active comments/orders.

Sourcing target math:

- Build a raw list of 80-120 prospects.
- Contact 50-70 prospects.
- Qualify 25-35 interested sellers.
- Onboard 15-25 sellers.
- Approve 10-20 launch vendors.
- Launch with 100-300 SKUs, assuming 8-15 approved SKUs per active vendor.

## Pipeline Stages

1. Sourced: prospect identified with category and contact path.
2. Contacted: first outreach sent.
3. Interested: seller replied positively or accepted intro call/chat.
4. Qualified: rubric completed and no launch-blocking risk found.
5. Onboarding started: checklist shared and seller committed to submit materials.
6. Documents submitted: business profile, identity/business documents, payout details, policies, and catalog files received.
7. Catalog review: products, images, prices, stock, and policy fit under review.
8. Test order: internal order placed or simulated operationally to validate communication, packing, dispatch, and status updates.
9. Approved for launch: seller and SKUs approved, launch incentive accepted, go-live date confirmed.
10. Live: seller products visible and seller is in weekly activation reporting.
11. Nurture: not ready for launch but may be reconsidered later.
12. Rejected: trust, quality, fulfillment, or policy risk.

Exit criteria for `Approved for launch`:

- Qualification score meets threshold.
- Required documents and payout details are complete.
- Minimum launch catalog is approved.
- Seller accepts service level expectations and launch terms.
- Test order passes or operational owner signs off on a justified exception.

We are selecting a small first cohort of sellers with clear products, reliable fulfillment, and good customer response. Launch sellers can list approved products, join early promotions, and get onboarding support.

Would you be open to a short chat this week about adding 5-20 of your best products to Mercato?

### WhatsApp / Instagram Follow-up

Hi [Name], following up on Mercato launch seller onboarding.

For the first cohort, we are looking for:

- 5-20 launch-ready products
- Clear prices, stock, and product photos
- Dispatch within the agreed timeline
- Basic seller details and payout information

If you are interested, send your best product list or catalog link and I will review fit today.

### Small Shop Outreach

Hi [Name], we are onboarding selected local shops for Mercato, a new marketplace focused on approved sellers and reviewed listings.

Your shop could be a fit if you can provide a small launch catalog, product photos/prices, stock counts, and reliable order dispatch.

The first step is a short qualification review. If approved, we help prepare your seller profile, catalog, and test order before launch. Who is the right person to discuss this with?

### Referral Ask

Hi [Name], we are building the first vendor cohort for Mercato and looking for reliable sellers in [category].

Do you know any shop owners, Instagram sellers, WhatsApp sellers, or small brands with good products and dependable fulfillment? Ideal sellers have 5-30 products ready, clear photos/prices, and respond quickly.

Please send an introduction or their contact details if they are open to a new marketplace channel.

### Interested Seller Qualification Prompt

Thanks for your interest. To check launch fit, please share:

1. Business/store name and main contact.
2. Product category and 5-20 products you want to launch with.
3. Product photos, prices, stock counts, and variants if available.
4. Dispatch timeline after an order is placed.
5. Return/exchange policy.
6. Payout account details can come later during onboarding.

We approve only launch-ready sellers, so clear product details and fast replies matter.

### Rejection / Nurture Message

Thanks for sharing your details. For this first Mercato launch cohort, we are only approving sellers who are ready with complete product content, reliable fulfillment, and the required onboarding details.

You are not selected for the launch cohort right now because [reason]. We can reconsider after launch if [specific improvement].

## Seller Onboarding Checklist

### Business Profile

- Store name
- Primary contact name
- Phone / WhatsApp
- Email
- Business address or operating city
- Social/store links
- Category focus
- Short store description
- Customer support contact
- Fulfillment contact if different

### KYC / Business Documents

Required for launch MVP:

- Government identity or business registration document, depending on seller type.
- Proof of active selling presence when available: shop photo, social storefront, marketplace profile, invoice sample, supplier proof, or customer order history.
- Signed acceptance of Mercato seller terms, marketplace policies, prohibited products, dispute process, and launch cohort terms.

Escalate to leadership before approval when:

- Seller offers branded goods without sourcing proof.
- Category has health, safety, legal, or counterfeit risk.
- Identity or business details do not match payout details.
- Seller asks to bypass product review, return policy, or test order.

### Bank / Payout Details

- Account holder name
- Bank or wallet provider
- Account number / IBAN / wallet number as applicable
- Payout contact
- Preferred payout schedule
- Tax or invoice details if required
- Confirmation that payout details match seller identity or approved business entity

### Product Catalog

Required fields per SKU:

- Product name
- Category
- SKU or internal product code
- Price
- Sale price if applicable
- Stock quantity
- Variants such as size, color, model, or pack count
- Product description
- Key specifications
- Product images
- Return eligibility
- Dispatch time
- Warranty or guarantee details, if any
- Restricted claims review, if applicable

Minimum launch catalog:

- 5 approved SKUs per vendor for inclusion.
- Preferred: 8-15 approved SKUs per vendor.
- Maximum for first pass: 30 SKUs per vendor unless catalog quality is exceptional and review capacity is available.

### Images

- Main image should clearly show the actual product.
- Avoid misleading brand marks, copied marketplace images, watermarks that obscure the product, or heavily edited images.
- Require enough images to validate variants, package contents, and buyer expectations.
- Flag beauty, wellness, electronics, and branded products for extra claim and authenticity review.

### Policies

Seller must confirm:

- Dispatch SLA.
- Stock update responsibility.
- Return/exchange handling expectations.
- Defect, wrong item, and missing item process.
- Customer communication boundaries.
- Product accuracy responsibility.
- Consequences for repeated cancellations, late dispatch, or poor quality.

### Test Order

Before go-live, run a test order or operational simulation:

- Confirm product availability.
- Confirm seller acknowledges order within expected response window.
- Confirm packing and handoff process.
- Confirm dispatch timeline.
- Confirm tracking/status update path.
- Confirm issue escalation contact.
- Record pass/fail and corrective action.

### Go-live Approval

Vendor is approved for launch only when:

- Business profile complete.
- Documents accepted.
- Payout details captured.
- Catalog approved.
- Policies accepted.
- Test order passed or exception approved.
- Launch incentive and commitment terms accepted.
- Weekly reporting owner assigned.

## Launch Cohort Incentive

Recommended launch incentive:

- 0% commission for the first 30 days after seller go-live or first 20 completed orders, whichever comes first.
- Featured placement eligibility for sellers with complete catalogs, reliable stock, and no launch SLA breaches.
- Free onboarding support for product setup and first campaign participation.
- Early seller badge such as `Launch Seller` if the product/design team approves customer-facing badge language.

Rationale:

- Keeps seller acquisition friction low.
- Rewards fast activation without permanently weakening marketplace economics.
- Gives operations leverage to require catalog quality, responsiveness, and test-order completion.

## Seller Commitment Terms

Launch vendors must agree to:

- Provide accurate product information, images, pricing, stock, and dispatch timelines.
- Maintain enough stock for listed products or update stock before orders fail.
- Respond to Mercato operations within one business day during launch.
- Accept marketplace order, return, dispute, and support workflows.
- Participate in one launch promotion or featured collection if selected.
- Fulfill orders within the agreed SLA.
- Notify Mercato before pausing operations or changing major catalog/pricing terms.
- Accept removal or demotion for repeated late dispatch, cancellations, counterfeit risk, misleading content, or poor responsiveness.

Suggested launch SLA:

- Seller order acknowledgement: same day or next business day.
- Dispatch: 24-72 hours depending on category and local logistics reality.
- Stock correction after issue: within one business day.
- Support escalation response: within one business day.

## Activation KPIs

Track weekly from sourcing through the first 30 launch days:

Pipeline KPIs:

- Prospects sourced
- Prospects contacted
- Reply rate
- Interested sellers
- Qualified sellers
- Onboarding started
- Documents submitted
- Catalogs submitted
- Vendors approved
- Vendors live

Catalog KPIs:

- SKUs submitted
- SKUs approved
- SKU approval rate
- Average approved SKUs per vendor
- Rejected SKU reasons
- Product image/content completion rate

Activation KPIs:

- Sellers with at least 5 approved SKUs
- Sellers with at least 1 completed test order
- Sellers live by launch date
- Sellers with first customer order
- Seller response SLA compliance
- Cancellation rate
- Late dispatch rate
- Return/dispute rate by seller
- GMV and orders by seller once live

Launch success threshold:

- 10+ live approved vendors.
- 100+ approved launch SKUs.
- 80%+ sellers responding within one business day during onboarding.
- 90%+ approved sellers complete test order or approved operational simulation.
- No unresolved high-risk authenticity, payout, or policy exceptions at launch.

Cadence:

- Friday: launch readiness review, risks, and next-week sourcing priorities.

Weekly report sections:

- New prospects sourced

- Approved vendors and SKUs
- Onboarding blockers

Standing review questions:

- Are we on track for 10-20 vendors and 100-300 SKUs?
- Which categories are underfilled?
- Which vendors are high potential but blocked?
- Which vendors should be removed before they create buyer trust risk?

## Escalation Rules

Escalate to leadership before approval when:

- Seller has counterfeit, restricted goods, regulated claims, or document mismatch risk.
- Seller needs non-standard commission, payout, return, or exclusivity terms.
- Seller could materially improve launch assortment but fails one qualification area.
- Seller requests launch priority, featured placement, or customer-facing claims outside approved policy.

Move seller to nurture when:

- Seller is interested but has incomplete catalog or documents.
- Seller needs better photos, clearer prices, or stock cleanup.
- Seller cannot commit to launch timing but may fit post-launch.

Reject seller when:

- Trust risk is unresolved.
- Seller refuses documents, payout validation, product review, or policy terms.
- Seller cannot fulfill reliably.
- Seller repeatedly misses responses during onboarding.
- Seller catalog creates high buyer complaint risk.

Pause or remove live seller when:

- Two missed dispatch SLAs in a rolling 30-day launch window.
- Two avoidable cancellations from inaccurate stock.
- Any credible counterfeit or prohibited product concern until reviewed.
- Repeated customer support non-response.
- Material mismatch between listing and delivered product.

## Coordination With Technical Workstream

No code implementation is required for ECL-47. Operations should coordinate with technical owners only to confirm the current seller onboarding flow can support the launch checklist.

Technical coordination asks:

- Confirm where seller profile, document notes, payout details, catalog status, and approval status should be tracked for MVP.
- Confirm whether test orders should be real local orders, admin-created checks, or operational simulations.
- Confirm product approval states and required fields before launch sellers are marked live.
- Confirm whether `Launch Seller` or featured placement is available in CMS/admin controls before using it in seller promises.
- Confirm export/reporting path for weekly vendor KPIs.

## 30-Day Execution Plan

Week 1:

- Build raw prospect list of 80-120 sellers.
- Send first outreach to 50+ prospects.
- Complete qualification calls/chats for 10-15 interested sellers.
- Select first 8-12 sellers for onboarding start.
- Validate seller checklist with finance/admin/support owners.

Week 2:

- Continue sourcing to fill category gaps.
- Start onboarding for 15-20 sellers.
- Collect documents, payout details, and initial catalogs.
- Begin catalog review and image/content cleanup.
- Run first test orders or simulations.

Week 3:

- Approve first 8-12 vendors.
- Complete 100+ approved SKUs.
- Resolve category gaps with targeted outreach.
- Confirm launch incentive acceptance.
- Identify sellers at risk and move weak sellers to nurture or reject.

Week 4:

- Finalize 10-20 launch vendors.
- Finalize 100-300 approved SKUs.
- Complete test order evidence.
- Lock weekly reporting sheet.
- Confirm go-live dates, launch promotion participation, and escalation contacts.

## Deliverables

- Vendor target list and pipeline tracker using the fields above.
- Qualification rubric applied to every launch candidate.
- Outreach message set for social, WhatsApp/Instagram, small shop, and referral channels.
- Seller onboarding checklist and go-live approval gate.
- Launch cohort incentive and commitment terms.
- Weekly KPI/reporting format.

## Final Recommendation

Mercato should launch with a smaller, tightly controlled vendor cohort rather than maximizing seller count. The operating target should be 10-20 vendors with clean catalogs, reliable fulfillment, fast communication, and clear approval evidence. This protects buyer trust while giving the marketplace enough assortment to look active at launch.
