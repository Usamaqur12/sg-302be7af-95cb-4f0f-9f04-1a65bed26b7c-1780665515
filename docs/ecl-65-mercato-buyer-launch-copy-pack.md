## Purpose

This pack gives Mercato buyer-facing launch copy for the controlled launch stage. It is written for a trust-first marketplace with approved sellers, reviewed products, order tracking, support routing and controlled deals.

Use this copy only where the underlying product and operations flow is ready. Do not use it to imply instant refunds, absolute seller quality, fixed delivery speed, lowest prices, fixed stock availability or broad buyer protection beyond approved policy.

Launch positioning line:

Approved sellers. Reviewed products. Clear deals.

Supporting message:

Short version:

Shop trusted sellers and reviewed products on Mercato.

## Copy Guardrails

Use:

- Approved sellers
- Reviewed products
- Clear deals
- Order tracking
- Practical support
- Clear return and support steps
- Controlled launch categories
- Limited-time launch offers from approved sellers

Avoid:

- Refund certainty unless support and finance policy explicitly approves the claim
- Delivery certainty unless confirmed by operations
- Lowest-price certainty
- Item-authenticity certainty for every item unless verification exists
- Instant approval, instant payout or seller promises in buyer copy
- Direct comparison to named competitors or other marketplaces
- Any payment method claim unless that payment method is live and tested

### Approved Seller Standards

Mercato sellers must complete the marketplace approval process before selling. Approval may include business details, store setup, product readiness and fulfillment expectations.

This does not remove every shopping risk, but it creates a stronger starting point than an open, unreviewed listing environment.

### Product Listing Review

Product pages should help buyers understand what is being sold, who is selling it, what it costs, whether it is in stock and what expectations apply.

Mercato may review products before they appear publicly or before they are included in launch campaigns. Listings that are unclear, restricted, misleading or unsupported can be rejected or removed.

### Order Visibility

Buyers can use Mercato order pages to check order status and available tracking information. Delivery timing can vary by seller, category, courier and payment method, so any delivery promise should come from the specific checkout or order details shown to the buyer.

### Support Path

If a buyer has an issue with an order, return, payment proof, seller response or product concern, Mercato support can review the case and route it according to approved support and finance rules.

Support decisions depend on the order record, seller response, payment state, delivery state, evidence and current marketplace policy.

### Buyer Responsibilities

Buyers help the marketplace work by:

- Reviewing product details before purchase.
- Using accurate contact and delivery information.
- Keeping order and payment proof where applicable.
- Reporting problems through the approved support path.
- Returning eligible items in the required condition and time window once policy is finalized.

### What Support Can Help With

Support can help with:

- Order status questions.
- Payment confirmation or manual proof routing where available.
- Seller response issues.
- Damaged, wrong, missing or not-as-described item reports.
- Return or refund questions once the order is eligible under approved policy.
- Seller-quality concerns.

### What Support Needs From Buyers

To review a case, support may ask for:

- Order number.
- Product and seller name.
- Delivery status.
- Photos or short videos for damaged, wrong or not-as-described items.
- Payment confirmation details where applicable.
- A clear description of the issue and preferred resolution.

### Policy Note

Return, refund and cancellation outcomes depend on final marketplace policy, item category, order state, seller response, payment status and evidence. Mercato should not publish refund certainty language until finance and support approval limits are confirmed.

## Buyer FAQ Copy

### What is Mercato?

Mercato is a multi-vendor marketplace where buyers can shop products from approved sellers, browse reviewed listings, follow order status and contact support when an order needs help.

### What does approved seller mean?

Approved sellers have completed Mercato's seller review process before selling or being promoted in launch collections. Approval helps create clearer standards, but buyers should still review each product page before ordering.

### Are all products reviewed?

Mercato may review products before they go live or before they are included in promoted launch collections. Product pages should include clear titles, images, descriptions, prices and stock details.

### How do I track my order?

Use your Mercato account order page to check the latest available status and tracking details. Delivery timing can vary by seller, courier, location and payment method.

### Can I return an item?

Return eligibility depends on category, order state, item condition, evidence and approved marketplace policy. Check the product/order details and contact support if your order needs review.

### How do I contact support?

Use the Mercato support path and include your order number, product name, seller name and a clear description of the issue. For damaged, wrong or not-as-described items, keep the packaging and share clear photos where requested.

### Can launch deals change?

No. Launch offers can change by seller, stock, campaign timing and product availability. Always check the current product page before checkout.

- Payment method claims match the live checkout configuration.
- Return/refund page is aligned with final support and finance approval limits.
- Category pages have enough approved in-stock SKUs before paid demand is scaled.
