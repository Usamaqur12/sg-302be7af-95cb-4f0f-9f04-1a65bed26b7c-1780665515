## Use Rules

These assets are for Mercato seller recruitment, onboarding, support and launch activation.

- Use Mercato-owned trust-first marketplace language.
- Do not compare Mercato to other marketplaces or retailers.
- Do not promise guaranteed traffic, guaranteed sales, instant approval, instant payout, fastest delivery or guaranteed refunds.
- State clearly that seller approval and product approval are required before products can be promoted.
- Keep payout, payment, delivery and return wording aligned with current operations policy before publishing.

## Asset 1: Seller One-Pager

### Headline

Sell on Mercato with an approved storefront, reviewed products and practical marketplace support.

### Short Positioning

Mercato is a managed multi-vendor marketplace for approved sellers who want to list quality products, reach shoppers through category discovery and participate in controlled launch campaigns.

### Who Should Apply

Mercato is a fit for sellers who:

- Have real inventory available for sale.
- Can provide clear product information, prices, stock counts and product images.
- Can fulfill orders within the delivery expectations they share with Mercato.
- Can support returns, customer questions and order issues through the marketplace process.
- Sell products that fit Mercato launch categories such as electronics, fashion, home, beauty, wellness and other approved retail categories.

Mercato may not be a fit for sellers who cannot confirm product availability, use misleading product claims, do not have fulfillment readiness or cannot follow marketplace approval and support rules.

### What Sellers Get

- A Mercato seller profile and storefront path after approval.
- Product listing tools for approved categories.
- Seller Center access for products, orders, finance, marketing, analytics, account health and support.
- Eligibility for launch campaigns, vouchers, bundles, featured placements and seller spotlights after approval.
- Marketplace guidance for listing quality, campaign readiness and seller account health.

### Approval Flow

1. Submit the seller registration form with business, owner and contact details.
2. Provide requested business, identity, payout or category documents if operations requires them.
3. Mercato reviews the seller application for category fit, readiness and trust requirements.
4. After seller approval, set up the store profile and submit products for review.
5. Mercato reviews products before they go live or become eligible for promotion.
6. Approved sellers with approved products may be invited to join launch campaigns.

### Seller Trust Rules

- Products must be accurate, available and priced clearly.
- Product titles, images and descriptions must match what the buyer receives.
- Sellers must not use unsupported claims, fake scarcity, misleading discounts or competitor comparisons.
- Returns, support and delivery expectations must match Mercato policy and seller capability.
- Campaign participation requires an approved seller account and approved products.

### Primary Call To Action

Apply to become a Mercato seller and prepare your first product listings for review.

## Asset 2: Seller Onboarding Checklist

### Before Registration

- Confirm the business name, owner name, email and phone number.
- Select the main product category or categories for launch.
- Prepare a short store description that explains what the seller offers.
- Prepare a store logo and banner if available.
- Gather business, identity, tax, KYC or category documents if operations asks for them.
- Confirm payout or bank information required by the current finance workflow.
- Confirm the return address, shipping regions and fulfillment method.

### Registration And Approval

- Submit the seller registration form.
- Use a reachable email and phone number for document or approval follow-up.
- Respond to missing-information requests with complete details.
- Wait for seller approval before expecting products to be promoted or visible in launch campaigns.
- Review Mercato seller guidelines before submitting the first products.

### Store Setup

- Add the store name, logo, banner and short description.
- Keep the store description factual and buyer-friendly.
- Avoid unsupported claims such as fastest shipping, guaranteed lowest prices or guaranteed results.
- Confirm support contact routing through Mercato's seller support process.
- Check that return and delivery expectations match current operations policy.

### First Product Batch

Prepare 5 to 10 products for initial review.

For each product, include:

- Product title.
- Category.
- Brand or manufacturer when applicable.
- Accurate description.
- Clear product images.
- Price and any real discount.
- Stock count.
- Variants such as size, color, model or capacity where relevant.
- Shipping, return or handling notes required by the category.

### Fulfillment And Support Readiness

- Confirm that listed stock is available before submitting products.
- Define packing and dispatch steps for each product type.
- Keep expected handling time realistic.
- Prepare a process for customer questions, order issues and return requests.
- Confirm who on the seller team monitors Mercato order and support updates.

### Campaign Readiness

- Join launch campaigns only with approved products.
- Confirm discount, voucher, bundle or featured-placement details before campaign submission.
- Do not submit products that are pending review, rejected, out of stock or unable to meet fulfillment expectations.
- Keep campaign claims specific and enforceable.

### Internal Activation Checklist

Mercato team checklist:

- Seller registration received.
- Application reviewed for category fit and readiness.
- Missing document request sent if required.
- Seller approval or rejection recorded.
- Store profile checked for completeness.
- First product batch reviewed for image, title, price, stock and category quality.
- Product feedback sent when listings need revision.
- Approved products considered for launch campaign eligibility.
- Seller receives activation email or campaign invitation where appropriate.

## Asset 3: Product Listing Quality Guide

### Listing Standard

Every product listing should help a buyer understand exactly what is being sold, who sells it, what it costs, whether it is available and what support or return expectations apply.

### Product Titles

Use clear, searchable titles.

Recommended format:

`Brand or product name + key model/type + important attribute + size/color/capacity`

Good examples:

- `AeroFit Wireless Earbuds, Noise Reduction, Black`
- `Cotton Bath Towel Set, 4 Pieces, White`
- `Stainless Steel Water Bottle, 750 ml, Blue`

Avoid:

- ALL CAPS titles.
- Keyword stuffing.
- Claims like `best`, `number one`, `guaranteed`, `official` or `lowest price` unless formally approved and provable.
- Competitor names or marketplace comparisons.

### Images

Product images should be clear enough for a buyer to inspect the item.

Minimum guidance:

- Use sharp, well-lit images.
- Show the actual product, not only packaging or a decorative scene.
- Include front, back, side and detail images when useful.
- Show variants accurately.
- Avoid watermarks, misleading overlays, unrelated objects or text-heavy promotional images.
- Do not use images that misrepresent size, quantity, brand or included accessories.

### Descriptions

Write product descriptions in direct buyer language.

Include:

- What the product is.
- Main materials, specs, size or compatibility details.
- What is included in the box or package.
- Care, use or setup notes when relevant.
- Category-specific warnings, restrictions or handling notes when required.

Avoid:

- Unsupported health, safety, performance or income claims.
- Fake urgency.
- Promises about guaranteed refunds, fastest delivery or guaranteed outcomes.
- Copy copied from another seller unless the seller has the right to use it.

### Pricing And Discounts

- Enter the regular selling price accurately.
- Use discounts only when the seller can honor them.
- Do not inflate a reference price to create a misleading discount.
- Confirm bundle pricing before campaign submission.
- Keep taxes, shipping and handling language aligned with current Mercato operations policy.

### Stock And Availability

- List only products that are available to sell.
- Keep stock counts updated.
- Pause or update products that cannot be fulfilled.
- Do not submit out-of-stock products for campaigns.
- Confirm variant-level stock for products with sizes, colors or models.

### Category Fit

- Choose the most accurate category for each product.
- Do not submit restricted, unclear or unsupported product types without operations approval.
- Use category attributes where available.
- Group variants under the right parent product when supported by the product workflow.

### Prohibited Or High-Risk Claims

Do not use claims that Mercato cannot verify or enforce, including:

- Guaranteed sales, traffic or ranking.
- Instant approval or instant payout.
- Fastest delivery or guaranteed delivery timing unless operations has approved the exact promise.
- Guaranteed refunds outside the published return policy.
- Medical, safety, certification or compliance claims without documentation.
- Competitor comparisons.
- Fake reviews, ratings or endorsements.

### Submission Review

Before submitting a product, check:

- Title is clear and specific.
- Images match the product and variant.
- Description matches what the buyer receives.
- Price and stock are accurate.
- Category is correct.
- Return, shipping and support notes are realistic.
- Any discount or campaign claim can be enforced.

## Asset 4: Seller FAQ

### What is Mercato?

Mercato is a managed multi-vendor marketplace where approved sellers can list reviewed products, manage orders through Seller Center and participate in controlled launch campaigns when eligible.

### Who can sell on Mercato?

Retailers, category specialists and local businesses can apply if they have real inventory, clear product information and the ability to fulfill orders and support buyers through Mercato's marketplace process.

### Is seller approval required?

Yes. Sellers must be approved before they can operate as Mercato sellers.

### Is product approval required?

Yes. Products are reviewed before they go live or become eligible for promotion. A seller approval does not automatically approve every product.

### How long does approval take?

Approval timing depends on application completeness, document requirements and operations capacity. Sellers should submit complete information and respond quickly to follow-up requests.

### What documents may be needed?

Mercato operations may request business, identity, category, tax, payout or KYC documents depending on the seller type and product category.

### Are there seller fees?

Seller fees, commissions, campaign charges or payout deductions should follow the current approved Mercato fee policy. Sellers should confirm the active fee terms before launch participation.

### When do sellers receive payouts?

Payout timing and method depend on the current finance workflow, order status, return handling and any manual review requirements. Mercato should not be described as offering instant payouts unless operations formally supports that flow.

### Can sellers join launch campaigns?

Approved sellers with approved products may be invited to participate in launch campaigns such as vouchers, bundles, flash deals or featured placements. Campaign participation is not guaranteed.

### What products are eligible for campaigns?

Campaign products must be approved, in stock, accurately priced and able to meet fulfillment and support expectations. Products that are pending review, rejected or out of stock should not be submitted for campaign placement.

### Can sellers promise discounts or limited offers?

Yes, if the discount or offer is accurate, approved and enforceable. Sellers should not use fake scarcity, inflated reference prices or unsupported claims.

### How are returns handled?

Returns should follow Mercato's current return policy and any category-specific operations rules. Sellers must provide accurate product information and support marketplace return handling when required.

### How are buyer support issues handled?

Sellers should monitor Mercato order and support updates and respond to questions or issues through the approved support path. Sellers should keep order, stock and fulfillment information current to reduce support problems.

### What happens if a product listing needs revision?

Mercato may send feedback when titles, images, descriptions, categories, prices, claims or stock details need correction. Sellers should revise and resubmit the listing for review.

### What is account health?

Account health reflects seller readiness and quality signals such as accurate listings, fulfillment reliability, support responsiveness, policy compliance and product quality. Poor account health may limit campaign eligibility or seller activity.

### Can sellers promote their Mercato storefront?

Yes. Sellers may share their Mercato storefront or approved products using accurate copy and approved campaign details. They should avoid unsupported claims and should not imply that unapproved products are available on Mercato.

### Who should sellers contact for help?

Sellers should use the Mercato seller support path for registration, approval, product review, orders, payouts, campaigns and account health questions.
