# ECL-48 Mercato Launch Positioning, Funnel, Vendor Kit And 30-Day Channel Plan

Date: 2026-06-16
Issue: ECL-48
Project: Amzn Project / Mercato Multi-Vendor Marketplace
Owner: Content Manager
Work mode: planning only

## Source Of Truth

This plan is grounded in the current Mercato repository and the existing ECL-23 and ECL-33 planning artifacts.

Verified platform signals:

- Mercato is positioned in-app as a controlled multi-vendor marketplace with buyer, seller and admin surfaces.
- Buyer launch surfaces already exist for homepage discovery, categories, product detail, deals, search, cart, checkout, wishlist, orders, order tracking, reviews, returns and support.
- Seller launch surfaces already exist for registration, Seller Center, products, orders, payouts, finance, marketing, learning, account health, analytics, support and store settings.
- Admin launch surfaces already exist for seller approval, product moderation, orders, payouts, promotions, marketing, CMS, support, settings and reports.
- ECL-33 already establishes broad launch growth and content rules: Mercato-owned copy, trust-first claims, no competitor lookalike branding and controlled paid acquisition until checkout, payment, seller approval and support workflows are verified.

## Launch Positioning

Primary positioning:

`Mercato is a trusted marketplace for approved sellers, reviewed products, clear deals and practical buyer support.`

Short version:

`Approved sellers. Reviewed products. Clear deals.`

Seller-facing positioning:

`Mercato gives growing retailers a managed storefront, product approval path, campaign tools and order/finance workflows in one Seller Center.`

Buyer-facing positioning:

`Mercato helps shoppers discover deals from approved sellers with order tracking, returns and support built into the marketplace.`

## Message Pillars

Trust:

- Approved sellers and reviewed listings.
- Clear return, support and order tracking paths.
- Admin-controlled marketplace quality.
- No unsupported promises around fastest delivery, guaranteed refunds or instant payouts.

Value:

- Daily deals, featured products, bundles, vouchers and new arrivals.
- Campaigns that are limited to approved sellers and approved products.
- Category-led discovery for electronics, fashion, home, beauty and wellness.

Seller Opportunity:

- Seller Center for registration, store setup, products, orders, marketing, analytics, account health and finance.
- Campaign and promotion submissions from the seller workspace.
- Marketplace guidance through learning, assortment growth and account health pages.

Operational Readiness:

- Manual payment and payout messaging should stay clear until provider integrations are selected.
- Buyer acquisition should scale only after checkout, payment confirmation, support, returns and category supply depth are verified.
- Seller acquisition should scale only when the approval SLA and product moderation queue can support new demand.

## Audience Strategy

Primary buyer audiences:

- Deal seekers who compare value and discounts before buying.
- Category shoppers in electronics, home, fashion, beauty and wellness.
- Trust-sensitive first-time marketplace buyers who need seller and support clarity.
- Repeat shoppers who respond to wishlist, order update and weekly deals messaging.

Primary seller audiences:

- Small retailers with real inventory and basic fulfillment capability.
- Category specialists with product depth in one or two launch categories.
- Local businesses that need a marketplace storefront without building a full ecommerce site.
- Marketplace-aware sellers who understand vouchers, campaigns and listing quality.

## Funnel Plan

### Buyer Funnel

Awareness:

- Homepage hero and launch announcement.
- Category-led organic content.
- Social product reels and deal posts.
- Small paid search tests around category and deal intent after production readiness gates pass.

Consideration:

- Category pages with clear filters and approved-product inventory.

Conversion:

Retention:

- Review request after delivery.
- Wishlist and price/drop reminders.

Consideration:

Conversion:

Retention:

## Vendor Kit

The launch vendor kit should be a concise set of reusable materials for seller recruitment, onboarding and activation.

Kit components:

| Asset | Purpose | Owner | Launch Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Seller one-pager | Explain Mercato, who should sell, approval flow and benefits | Content | P0 |
| Seller onboarding checklist | Give sellers a step-by-step registration and first-listing path | Content/Ops | P0 |
| Product listing quality guide | Standardize titles, images, descriptions, pricing and inventory | Content/Ops | P0 |
| Campaign participation guide | Explain vouchers, flash deals, bundles and featured placements | Marketing/Ops | P1 |
| Seller FAQ | Handle fees, approval, payouts, returns, support and product moderation | Content/Ops | P0 |
| Outreach email sequence | Recruit and activate sellers through 3-5 messages | Content | P0 |
| WhatsApp/DM scripts | Support direct local seller recruitment without ad-hoc claims | Content/Sales | P1 |
| Seller spotlight template | Capture seller story, category, products and promotion angle | Content | P2 |

Vendor kit copy rules:

- Use Mercato's own marketplace language.
- Do not compare directly to Amazon, Daraz, eBay, Walmart, Alibaba or other competitors in seller-facing public copy.
- Be explicit that products require approval before public sale unless product policy changes.
- Do not promise guaranteed traffic, guaranteed sales, instant approval or instant payout.
- Explain manual payout/payment realities plainly until automated provider flows are implemented.

## Vendor Onboarding Checklist

Seller-ready checklist:

1. Business name, owner name, email and phone are ready.
2. Store logo, banner and short store description are prepared.
3. Seller category focus is selected.
4. KYC/business documents are available if required by operations.
5. Bank or payout information is ready if the finance workflow requires it.
6. Product titles, descriptions, prices, stock and images are ready.
7. Return address, shipping region and fulfillment expectations are confirmed.
8. Seller has read product approval, return, support and campaign rules.

Internal activation checklist:

1. Seller registration received.
2. Seller approval queue reviewed.
3. Missing document request sent if needed.
4. Store profile checked for completeness.
5. First 5-10 products reviewed for launch category fit.
6. Product quality feedback sent if listings need repair.
7. Approved products added to launch campaign consideration.
8. Seller receives campaign invitation or first-week activation email.

## 30-Day Channel Plan

### Days 1-7: Foundation And Soft Launch

Goal: publish trust, seller and category foundations before broad acquisition.

- Finalize homepage launch copy, seller CTA copy and trust page copy.
- Publish seller one-pager, onboarding checklist, seller FAQ and listing quality guide.
- Recruit first launch sellers through direct outreach and existing networks.
- Prepare category landing copy for the strongest stocked categories.
- Post launch announcement, seller invitation and first deal/category social content.
- Start email flows for seller registration received, approval, product submitted and buyer welcome.
- Confirm tracking events for registration, seller application, product view, add to cart, checkout and order.

Success checks:

- Trust pages are live and linked.
- Vendor kit P0 assets are complete.
- Seller approval SLA target is defined.
- At least 3 launch categories have enough approved inventory for promotion.

### Days 8-14: Launch Campaign Activation

Goal: activate approved sellers and begin controlled buyer demand.

- Launch `Grand Opening Deals` collection with approved products only.
- Invite approved sellers to submit launch vouchers, bundles or flash deals.
- Publish 2 buyer category guides and 2 seller education posts.
- Run small organic and boosted social posts for strongest categories.
- Start seller recruitment email/DM sequence for priority categories.
- Create retargeting audiences, but keep spend low until checkout/payment proof is verified.

Success checks:

- Campaign products meet image, stock, discount and seller approval rules.
- Seller activation path is not blocked by moderation backlog.
- Buyer support and return links are visible on key pages.

### Days 15-21: Scale What Converts

Goal: expand only channels with confirmed supply, conversion and operations readiness.

- Double down on top-performing category/deal posts.
- Publish first seller spotlight if the seller quality and fulfillment path are confirmed.
- Run search ads for category/deal intent terms with low daily caps.
- Send first weekly deals digest to opted-in buyers.
- Send seller campaign readiness guide to approved sellers.
- Review analytics for traffic source, product views, carts, checkout starts, orders and seller applications.

Success checks:

- Paid channels have order or seller-application signal before budget increases.
- Seller/product approval queues remain within SLA.
- Category pages promoted in ads have enough live approved SKUs.

### Days 22-30: Optimize And Prepare Month 2

Goal: turn launch learning into a repeatable monthly channel rhythm.

- Publish buyer trust explainer and order tracking/returns content.
- Refresh homepage/category modules around actual winning categories and deals.
- Prepare Month 2 campaign calendar: Category Week, Seller Spotlight and Payday Value Picks.
- Build seller segment list: pending approval, approved/no products, approved/products/no campaign, active sellers.
- Send targeted seller nudges based on activation stage.
- Produce launch report with channel, funnel, seller and content metrics.

Success checks:

- Month 2 channel plan is based on actual funnel data.
- Weak categories are paused from paid promotion until supply improves.
- Seller activation blockers are listed for operations/product follow-up.

## Channel Cadence

Organic social:

- 1 product/deal post per day.
- 3 short videos per week.
- 2 seller education posts per week.
- 1 trust/support explainer per week.
- 1 seller spotlight per week after quality proof exists.

SEO/content:

- 2 buyer category guides per week.
- 1 seller education article per week.
- 1 trust/operations explainer per week.
- Internal links from homepage, category pages, deals, seller pages and trust pages.

Email:

- Buyer welcome, order updates, review prompt and weekly deals digest.
- Seller registration, approval/rejection, product status, campaign invitation and account health messages.
- Marketing emails require opt-in and unsubscribe handling.

Paid:

- Start with low-cap tests only.
- Avoid competitor trademark bidding and lookalike copy.
- Pause ads for any category with weak supply or operational blockers.

## Measurement Plan

North-star launch measures:

- Approved sellers with live products.
- Approved live SKUs by category.
- Buyer checkout completion rate.
- Orders with successful payment confirmation.
- Seller applications by source.
- First product approval time.
- Product moderation backlog age.
- Support/return contact rate after order.

Channel metrics:

- Organic: impressions, clicks, product views, seller CTA clicks.
- SEO/content: indexed pages, search clicks, category guide clicks, assisted product views.
- Email: send, open, click, unsubscribe, registration/order/campaign actions.
- Paid: CPC, CTR, product view rate, cart rate, checkout rate, seller application rate and cost per approved seller.
- Seller funnel: application start, application submit, approval, first product submitted, first product approved, first campaign request and first order.

## Gating And Pause Rules

Buyer demand should not scale if:

- Checkout or payment confirmation is not reliable in production.
- Support, returns or order tracking pages are incomplete or misleading.
- The promoted category has fewer than 20 approved live SKUs.
- Product images or prices are not trustworthy enough for ads.

Seller demand should not scale if:

- Seller approval SLA exceeds 48 hours.
- Product moderation SLA exceeds 72 hours.
- KYC/document handling is unclear.
- Payout/payment messaging is not approved by operations.

Campaigns should not launch if:

- Products are pending, rejected or out of stock.
- Seller is not approved.
- Discount or campaign claims cannot be enforced by admin tools.
- Return/support policy for the category is unclear.
